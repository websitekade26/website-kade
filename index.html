03 / How It Works

From chat to launch
in four steps.

A simple process that moves fast without breaking things. You always know what happens next.

Start broad, narrow with purpose
You bring the idea.
We handle the rest.

One WhatsApp message is enough. We sketch directions, you pick what clicks, we refine until it is right, then we ship it sharp.

Ask Us for Free

01Share the requirement

A 15-minute chat on WhatsApp or call. Your business, your customers, examples you like, budget and deadline. A straight answer on whether the studio is the right fit.

02Approve the direction

Two or three visual directions, not fifty confusing options. The look gets locked: colours, type, feel. No design-by-committee pain.

03Design and build

Content structure, mobile-first design, then build in custom code or WordPress. A preview link shows progress as it grows.

04Launch and support

Testing, speed pass, launch and search setup. Optional care plan after: updates, backups and small tweaks on WhatsApp.
